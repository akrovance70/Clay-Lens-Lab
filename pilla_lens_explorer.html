How the scores work

Ratings are not Pilla scores and are not scientific eye-color performance measurements. They are screening heuristics from stated VLT, dealer-listed shooting conditions and background descriptions. A score of 5/5 means a potential fit in that condition, not a better lens universally. Blue eyes alone do not establish a particular tint preference.

Direct sun: lower VLT scores higher because it blocks more light. Overcast: higher VLT and published flat/low-light use score higher. Dormant brown backgrounds: open-field/desert suitability and dealer-listed amber, orange, red or brown profile score higher. This last score is the least validated and should not be mistaken for tested target contrast against dormant vegetation.

Legend: ED = Enhanced Definition; HC = High Contrast; MX = Max Orange; N = Neutralizer; AR = Anti-reflective.
